Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from Central Illinois homeowners.

Do you help with homeowner insurance claims for storm damage? ▼

Yes, absolutely. Central Illinois experiences frequent severe storms with wind and hail. Justin Fry provides a thorough photo inspection report documenting lifted shingles, bruised granules, and gutter damage, and can meet your insurance adjuster on-site to verify proper claim compensation.

How long does a typical roof replacement take? ▼

Most residential single-family roofs in Vandalia and Cowden are completed in 1 to 2 days, weather permitting. We strip, inspect decking, apply ice barrier, and install new shingles efficiently while keeping your property clean.

What is the difference between stamped concrete and regular concrete? ▼

Regular concrete is typically finished with a brushed broom texture for traction (great for driveways and garage slabs). Stamped concrete is colored with integrated pigments and textured with pattern mats (slate, stone, cobblestone) to give the appearance of high-end natural stone at a fraction of the cost.

Are you licensed, bonded, and insured? ▼

Yes. CBT Construction is fully licensed, bonded, and carries general liability and workers' compensation coverage to protect homeowners across Fayette, Effingham, and Shelby counties.
